How This Tool Works

1

Budget Versions

Every set of budget numbers is saved as a named version (e.g. "Original Budget", "Q3 Revised"). Use the version bar at the top to switch versions, create a new one, duplicate the current one as a starting point, or delete one you no longer need. "Save" writes whatever is currently on screen to the selected version.

2

Entering a Budget

Open a channel tab and type monthly numbers into the Budget Sales and Budget Ad Spend rows. Actuals and variance update as you type, and nothing is saved until you click "Save" in the version bar.

3

Where Actuals Come From

Actual sales and ad spend for Amazon, Walmart, TikTok, DTC, and Whole Foods on Amazon are pulled from Triple Whale and cached here — ask for a refresh any time you want that cache updated. Instacart is the exception: it reads live from its own auto-syncing data source on every page load, so it never needs a manual refresh. Walmart and TikTok ad spend, and TikTok sales, show as "—" until those sources are connected in Triple Whale — that's a data-availability gap, not a $0 result.

4

Channel Mapping

Amazon = Amazon Ads spend (Every Body Eat account only) vs Amazon marketplace sales. Whole Foods on Amazon = the separate "WFMOA" Amazon Ads account's spend vs its ad-attributed sales (no separate WFMOA marketplace order feed exists, so Actual Sales here is the ad platform's own attributed-sales number, not order revenue — same for Instacart below). Walmart = Walmart marketplace sales (no ad spend source connected yet). TikTok = reserved for TikTok Shop sales and TikTok Ads spend, once connected. DTC = Meta + Google ad spend vs Shopify storefront sales (excludes Amazon-, Walmart-, and WFMOA-sourced orders). Instacart = Instacart Ads (Sponsored Products + Sponsored Display) spend vs ad-attributed sales, live from its own data source.
