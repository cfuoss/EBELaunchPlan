Methodology

Pulled all Sponsored Products keyword-level rows via the sponsored_product_keyword source on /api/v3/charts/tables, across every ad group in the account, for 4 timeframe windows (Last 30 Days, Last 90 Days, Last 6 Months, All Time / 14 Months) so the tables/charts above can be filtered by recency. Each keyword's actual text was read and tagged into one or more groups per dimension (a keyword like "gluten free pretzel snacks for kids" lands in Dietary → Gluten-Free, Occasion → Kids' Snacks/Lunchbox, and Product Format → Pretzels simultaneously).
  • Keywords with less than $10 in spend within the selected timeframe are excluded, to keep the dimension breakdowns focused on keywords with real signal rather than one-off auto-targeting noise. This is applied independently per timeframe, so a keyword can appear in "All Time" but not "Last 30 Days" if its recent spend is below the threshold.
  • The keyword-level report requires filtering by ad_group.id; 2 of 47 ad groups returned "ad group not found" (likely stale/deleted ad groups) and are excluded — a small, low-impact gap.
  • Rows include both manually-added keywords and Instacart's auto-targeting search-term matches, since both come back through this source — that's why the "No <X> Mentioned" bucket is common in several dimensions (plain category terms, not dietary/occasion/flavor-qualified searches).
  • ROAS and NTB% here are keyword-attributed, computed from this dataset's own spend/sales — they won't exactly match account-level dashboard totals, which include non-keyword-attributed spend too.
  • CTR is estimated by back-solving impressions from the API's per-row CTR and click count, then re-aggregating click-weighted — small rounding differences vs. Ads Manager are expected.
  • Spend and Sales are rounded to the nearest dollar for display; ROAS and NTB% are computed from full-precision underlying values before rounding.
