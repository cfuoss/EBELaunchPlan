How This Tool Works

1

Source of Truth

Everything lives in one place — this tool. Every channel tab reads from the same underlying data, so there's nothing to keep in sync manually.

2

Adding a Promotion

Click "+ Add a Promotion" at the top of the sidebar, fill in the fields — including which Channel it belongs to — and save. It appears immediately in that channel's tab.

3

Channel Tabs

Amazon, Walmart, TikTok, DTC, Retail, Distributor, and Other each get their own tab with a Gantt Calendar, a Promotions Table, and an Analysis section — scoped to just that channel's promotions.

4

Editing or Removing a Promotion

In any channel's Table, click "Edit" on a row to change its details, or "Delete" to remove it.

5

Running the Analysis

In a channel's Analysis section, type Baseline $, Promo $, Post Promo $, and True Cost directly into the table cells — Lift %, Incremental Revenue, and ROI calculate live.

6

Gantt Calendar

Rows are retailer/site within that channel. Columns are Year / Month / Week. Click any bar to see that promotion's full details. Overlapping promotions on the same row stack into separate lanes.
