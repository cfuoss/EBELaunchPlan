▾ How Item Tracing Works

Business Central holds every sales, inventory, production, and procurement record we have. An Item Trace follows one lot number through that entire history — everywhere it came from, and everywhere it ended up — so instead of piecing it together by hand, you pull two reports out of Business Central and this tool turns them into a visual, clickable trace with a plain-English summary of what happened at each step.

→

Origin → Usage

Start from a raw material or packaging lot and trace forward — every production order it fed into, every finished-good lot it became, every customer it shipped to.

Use it to answer: "If this raw material lot has a problem, what finished products and customers were affected?"

←

Usage → Origin

Start from a finished good, production order, or shipment and trace backward — the vendor lots and raw material receipts that went into it.

Use it to answer: "Where did the ingredients in this specific batch come from?"

Pulling a trace from Business Central

  1. 1
    Log in to Business Central with your EBE O365 credentials at businesscentral.dynamics.com/WholeAndFree-Prod.
  2. 2
    Open Item Tracing. Click the search (magnifying glass) icon in the top right, type "Item Tracing," and select the Item Tracing task.
  3. 3
    Enter the Lot No. Every trace has to start from a lot number — use a raw material or packing lot for Origin → Usage, or a finished-good lot for Usage → Origin.
  4. 4
    Set the direction and trace. Set Trace Method to "Origin → Usage" or "Usage → Origin," set Show Components to "No," then click Trace.
  5. 5
    Export it. Once results appear, click the export icon (box with an arrow) and choose Open in Excel to download the full trace.
  6. 6
    Do it again for the other direction, then upload both files above — this tool needs both to show the complete picture.

Tip: personalize the Item Tracing columns first so Entry Type, Source Type, Source Name, Lot No., and Posting Date are all showing — that's what this tool reads from the export.

Glossary of terms used in Business Central
Item Ledger Entry
A database entry recording a lot to a document — either a sales order or a released production order.
RLPROD######
A Released Production Order number.
SO#######
A Sales Order number.
PPRCP######
Document number of a Purchase Receipt.
PSSHP#####
Posted Sales Shipment / Invoice.
GO-LIVE / Take On
Items loaded at the initial inventory start from MRPeasy.
Posting Date
The date the transaction was completed in the system.
Source Name
Source of record — for sales documents, the customer name; for purchases, the vendor name.
