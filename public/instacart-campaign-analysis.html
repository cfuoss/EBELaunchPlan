Spend & NTB% Correlation Analysis

✅
Acquire Campaigns Drive NTB%
Acquire campaigns account for 71% of this month's spend, and the average NTB% across those campaigns is well above the account average. Top performers: SP - Acquire - Grow - Thins - The Fresh Market (70.0%), SP - Auto - Acquire - Thins - All Stores (64.3%), SP - Auto - Acquire - Pretzels - All Stores (58.0%).
💡
Max Sales — Low NTB by Design
Max Sales campaigns like SP - Auto - Max Sales - 14oz Cheese-Less - Sam's Club (0.0%), SP - Auto - Max Sales - Thins - All Stores (16.6%) intentionally target repeat/high-intent shoppers, so low NTB% here isn't a problem — it's the tradeoff for driving volume and ROAS from existing customers.
⚠️
Efficiency Tradeoff at Scale
Looking at the 3 highest-spend months (avg ROAS 2.36x) vs the 3 lowest-spend months (avg ROAS 3.67x), ROAS tends to compress as monthly spend scales up — a normal diminishing-returns pattern, not an NTB% problem, since NTB% has stayed resilient even in the highest-spend month (2026-01: 49.9% NTB at $24,926 spend).
🚀
Path to Higher NTB%
The highest-NTB Acquire campaigns this month are SP - Acquire - Grow - Thins - The Fresh Market (70.0%), SP - Auto - Acquire - Thins - All Stores (64.3%), SP - Auto - Acquire - Pretzels - All Stores (58.0%). Shifting incremental budget toward these — rather than spreading evenly across all Acquire campaigns — is the fastest lever to push blended NTB% higher next month.

Key Observations

📈
Sales Pace vs Prior Year
Projected sales this month are ▼ 40.2% vs Aug 2025 — trending clear of last year's pace.
🎯
NTB% Trend
Current NTB% is running at 42.5%, below the Aug 2025 rate of 44.3% — a healthy signal that the Acquire-heavy mix (71% of spend) is doing its job of bringing in new-to-brand customers.
💰
ROAS Trend — Acquire Tradeoff
Blended ROAS this month (1.95x) reflects the heavy Acquire skew — Acquire campaigns prioritize NTB% over immediate ROAS, so a lower blended ROAS alongside strong NTB% is the expected, intended tradeoff.
🏆
Best Historical Benchmark
Jan 26 is the strongest month on record: $56,981 in sales at 2.29x ROAS and 49.9% NTB% — a useful target for what a well-scaled month can look like.
