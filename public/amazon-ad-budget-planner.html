How This Tool Works

1

The Bottoms-Up Model

Every month's budget starts from four inputs: Clicks, CPC (cost per click), Conversion Rate, and AOV (average order value). From those, the tool calculates Spend (Clicks × CPC), Ad Orders (Clicks × Conversion Rate), Ad Sales (Ad Orders × AOV), and ROAS (Ad Sales ÷ Spend).

2

Budget Versions

Every set of budget numbers is saved as a named version. Use the version bar to switch, create, duplicate, or delete versions. "Save" writes whatever is on screen — for every channel — to the selected version.

3

Where Actuals Come From

Amazon and Walmart: Clicks, CPC, Conversion Rate, Spend, Ad Orders, and Ad Sales come from Helium 10 Ads (14-day attribution), refreshed nightly — typically complete through yesterday. ROAS is calculated from those. Only the Every Body Eat accounts are included (not any other associated seller account).

4

Filling Data Gaps

Helium 10 has Amazon Ads history back to January 2026, so every month fills in automatically. Use the "Manual Actuals" tab only to override a month; a manual entry always takes priority over Helium 10 for that month, and clearing it reverts to Helium 10. The same Manual Actuals mechanism is available for Walmart.

5

Variance and % Difference

Variance is Actual minus Budget. For CPC and Spend, coming in under budget (a negative variance) is good, shown in green — the opposite of every other metric. % Difference is Variance divided by Budget.
